The Election Timeline

What happens when? Think of it like a massive national festival.

Before (Preparation)

The Election Commission (ECI) announces dates. Parties campaign to win your support. This is when you must register to vote!

During (Action)

Also known as Polling Day! Go to your local booth and cast your vote on an Electronic Voting Machine (EVM).

After (Result)

EVMs are secured. On "Counting Day," votes are tallied and the winner is officially announced!

How to Register to Vote

Getting your name on the VIP guest list (Electoral Roll).

18+

Eligibility

Must be an Indian citizen and 18 years of age or older.

The Magic Form

You need to fill out Form 6 to register as a new voter.

How to Apply

Online: Voter's Service Portal or Voter Helpline App.
Offline: Submit to your Booth Level Officer (BLO).

Wait for your EPIC! Once approved, you will receive your Voter ID card, officially called the Electors Photo Identity Card (EPIC).

The Voting Day Process

What actually happens when you arrive at the booth.

1

Find your Booth

Check your voting slip or the Voter Helpline App to find your designated Polling Station.

2

Verify your Identity

Show your Voter ID (or another approved government ID like Aadhaar/PAN) to the officer.

3

Get Inked

An officer will mark your left index finger with un-erasable purple ink. This proves you've voted!

4

Sign the Register

Sign your name or put your thumbprint on the voter register.

Cast Your Vote (EVM)

Walk into the private voting compartment.

  • Press the blue button next to your chosen candidate.
  • Listen for a loud "BEEP".
  • Look at the VVPAT machine (printer) for 7 seconds to confirm your vote was recorded correctly!

Crucial Reminders

Your Vote is Secret

You never have to tell anyone who you voted for.

"My vote doesn't matter"

False! Many elections are won by tiny margins. Your vote is the foundation of government.

No Booth Selfies

Photography inside the booth is strictly prohibited. Take your "I Voted" selfie outside showing your inked finger!
